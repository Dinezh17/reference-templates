Who we are

Omaginfo LLC is a telecommunications company focused on providing voice and messaging connectivity solutions to operators, carriers, and business partners across international markets. We specialize in VoIP services, A–Z voice termination, SMS routing, and global connectivity solutions tailored to meet the evolving needs of the telecom industry.

We believe successful telecommunications partnerships are built on reliability, competitive commercial offerings, and mutual trust. Our approach combines flexible connectivity options with a commitment to professional service and long-term collaboration. At Omaginfo LLC, we strive to help our partners expand their reach, optimize their connectivity, and explore new business opportunities in an increasingly connected world.

  • Reliability
  • Competitive offerings
  • Mutual trust
  • Long-term collaboration

Our Mission

To deliver dependable and cost-effective voice and messaging connectivity while building transparent, mutually beneficial relationships with telecom operators and business partners worldwide.

Our Vision

To become a trusted international telecommunications partner recognized for connectivity, commercial flexibility, service reliability, and lasting business relationships.
